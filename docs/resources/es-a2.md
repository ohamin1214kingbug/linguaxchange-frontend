A2 is where Spanish opens up in time. At A1 you lived in the present; now you can tell someone about last weekend, describe your childhood and make plans, still in simple sentences, on familiar topics.

## What you'll be able to do | short: I can… | kicker: Your goal
- [ ] Say what you did yesterday, last weekend or on holiday
- [ ] Describe what your life used to be like
- [ ] Talk about your plans for the weekend or next year
- [ ] Shop, ask about sizes and prices, and return something
- [ ] Explain a simple health problem at the pharmacy or doctor's
- [ ] Write a short message telling a friend your news

## Recent past: he comido | short: Recent past | kicker: Grammar · 1 of 5
The *pretérito perfecto* is **haber + participle**. In Spain it's used for anything that happened today, this week or "ever"; in most of Latin America the simple past (next section) covers this too.

| | haber | + participle |
|---|---|---|
| yo | he | hablado, comido, vivido |
| tú | has | hablado |
| él / ella / usted | ha | comido |
| nosotros | hemos | vivido |
| vosotros | habéis | hablado |
| ellos / ustedes | han | comido |

:::examples
- Hoy **he trabajado** mucho. // I've worked a lot today.
- ¿**Has estado** en México? // Have you ever been to Mexico?
- Todavía no **hemos comido**. // We haven't eaten yet.
:::

:::tip
Common irregular participles: **hecho** (done), **dicho** (said), **visto** (seen), **escrito** (written), **puesto** (put), **vuelto** (come back), **abierto** (opened).
:::

:::mistake
- He hacido los deberes. => He hecho los deberes.
:::

:::practice
1. Hoy ___ ido al gimnasio. (yo) = he
2. ¿___ visto la película? (tú) = Has
3. Nosotros no ___ terminado. = hemos
:::

## Simple past: comí | short: Simple past | kicker: Grammar · 2 of 5
The *pretérito indefinido* tells finished events at a specific time: yesterday, last year, in 2019.

| | hablar | comer / vivir |
|---|---|---|
| yo | hablé | comí, viví |
| tú | hablaste | comiste |
| él / ella / usted | habló | comió |
| nosotros | hablamos | comimos |
| vosotros | hablasteis | comisteis |
| ellos / ustedes | hablaron | comieron |

The most common verbs are irregular: **fui** (I went / I was), **tuve** (I had), **hice** (I did), **estuve** (I was), **pude** (I could), **dije** (I said).

:::examples
- Ayer **fui** al cine con Ana. // Yesterday I went to the cinema with Ana.
- El verano pasado **viajamos** a Perú. // Last summer we travelled to Peru.
- ¿Qué **hiciste** el sábado? // What did you do on Saturday?
:::

:::tip
**Ser** and **ir** share the same past: *fui* means both "I was" and "I went". The context always makes it clear.
:::

:::mistake
- Ayer he ido al cine. => Ayer fui al cine.
With a finished time like ayer, el año pasado or en 2019, use the simple past.
:::

:::practice
1. Ayer ___ (yo, comer) paella. = comí
2. ¿Dónde ___ (tú, estar) el lunes? = estuviste
3. Ellos ___ (hablar) con el profesor. = hablaron
4. El año pasado ___ (yo, ir) a Japón. = fui
:::

## Simple past or imperfect? | short: Imperfect | kicker: Grammar · 3 of 5
The *imperfecto* describes the background: what things were like, what used to happen, what was going on. The simple past tells the events.

| | Imperfecto (background) | Indefinido (event) |
|---|---|---|
| use | habits, descriptions, ongoing | single finished actions |
| hablar | hablaba, hablabas, hablaba… | hablé |
| ser / ir | era / iba | fui |
| example | Llovía y hacía frío. // It was raining and cold. | Entonces llegó el autobús. // Then the bus arrived. |

:::examples
- De niño **vivía** en un pueblo. // As a child I lived in a village. (used to)
- **Estaba** en casa cuando **llamó** mi madre. // I was at home when my mother called.
- Siempre **íbamos** a la playa en agosto. // We always went to the beach in August.
:::

:::tip
Think of a film: the imperfect is the scenery and what's happening in the background, the simple past is what happens next in the plot.
:::

:::mistake
- Cuando fui niño, viví en Madrid. => Cuando era niño, vivía en Madrid.
Childhood and "used to" describe a long background period, so they take the imperfect.
:::

:::practice
1. Cuando ___ pequeña, jugaba al fútbol. (ser) = era
2. ___ (llover) cuando salí de casa. = Llovía
3. Mientras cocinaba, ___ (sonar) el teléfono. = sonó
:::

## Object pronouns | short: Pronouns | kicker: Grammar · 4 of 5
Pronouns replace things already mentioned. They go **before** a conjugated verb, or attached to the end of an infinitive.

| | Direct (it, him, her) | Indirect (to him, to her) |
|---|---|---|
| me / you | me / te | me / te |
| him, her, it, you (usted) | lo / la | le |
| us / you all | nos / os | nos / os |
| them, you (ustedes) | los / las | les |

:::examples
- ¿El libro? **Lo** tengo yo. // The book? I have it.
- **Le** escribo un mensaje a Juan. // I'm writing Juan a message.
- Voy a comprar**lo**. // I'm going to buy it.
:::

:::tip
When two pronouns meet, the indirect one comes first, and **le / les** turns into **se** before lo / la: *Se lo doy* = I give it to him.
:::

:::mistake
- Le lo doy. => Se lo doy.
:::

:::practice
1. ¿La pizza? ___ como ahora. = La
2. ___ doy el regalo a María. (to her) = Le
3. El regalo, ___ doy mañana. (it, to her) = se lo
:::

## Plans: voy a… | short: Plans | kicker: Grammar · 5 of 5
For plans and the near future, use **ir a + infinitive**. It's the most common way to talk about the future in everyday Spanish.

:::examples
- **Voy a visitar** a mis padres el domingo. // I'm going to visit my parents on Sunday.
- ¿Qué **vas a hacer** en verano? // What are you going to do in the summer?
- **Va a llover** esta tarde. // It's going to rain this afternoon.
:::

:::mistake
- Voy visitar a mis padres. => Voy a visitar a mis padres.
Don't drop the a.
:::

## Your weekend | short: Conversation | kicker: Put it together
Telling a friend about the weekend, using both past tenses.

:::dialogue
- Pablo: ¿Qué tal el fin de semana? // How was your weekend?
- Ana: Genial. El sábado fui a Toledo con mi hermana. // Great. On Saturday I went to Toledo with my sister.
- Pablo: ¡Qué bien! ¿Qué tiempo hacía? // How nice! What was the weather like?
- Ana: Hacía sol, pero había mucha gente. // It was sunny, but there were lots of people.
- Pablo: ¿Y el domingo? // And on Sunday?
- Ana: Me quedé en casa porque estaba cansada. // I stayed at home because I was tired.
- Pablo: ¿Vas a volver a Toledo? // Are you going to go back to Toledo?
- Ana: Sí, voy a ir con mis padres en mayo. // Yes, I'm going to go with my parents in May.
:::

## Watch out for | short: Watch out | kicker: Common difficulties
**Two past tenses.** Choosing between the simple past and the imperfect takes months. Start with the film rule and keep reading short stories.

**Spain vs Latin America.** In Spain, *hoy he comido* is the norm for today; much of Latin America says *hoy comí*. Both are correct; follow the Spanish you hear.

:::mistake
- Gracias para la ayuda. => Gracias por la ayuda.
Por is for cause, exchange and "through": gracias por, por la mañana. Para is for purpose and destination: para ti, para Madrid.
:::
